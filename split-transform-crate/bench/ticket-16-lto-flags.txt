# The LTO flags rustc receives, by addon crate-type

Source: cargo build -p stylex_compiler_rs --release --all-features -v
under [profile.release] with lto = true and codegen-units = 1.

LTO runs only on a final artifact. A crate that also emits a reusable rlib
is not final, so rustc receives no -C lto for it and cargo prints no warning.
